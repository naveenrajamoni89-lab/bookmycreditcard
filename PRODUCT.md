# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Indian consumers exploring and comparing credit cards by issuer, spending needs, fees and benefits.
## Product Purpose
BookMyCreditCard helps visitors shortlist cards, compare terms and check basic eligibility.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React and Vite website with local data and Supabase, filters, comparison, eligibility and account flows. Redesign only bank and category pages. Preserve card data and public routes.
## Brand Commitments
Preserve BookMyCreditCard name, logo and blue identity. User requests neat professional pages with real bank logos and card imagery, built directly in code. Desktop navigation flyouts open right and fit the viewport.
## Evidence on Hand
Local bank logos, card artwork and card/editorial data in public and src/data.
## Product Principles
Readable fees and restrictions. Useful interactions. Catalogue-derived counts.
